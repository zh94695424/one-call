## Findings

### 1-未修复-中-C→B post-retirement recovery requires a time-valid old-K1 overlap that cannot exist after C is reached, so the primary fixed-B recovery path cannot satisfy its own reopening gate

- **入口/函数**: S1 §5 "Whole-cohort sequence and rollback boundary", step 7 "Recovery" — C→B retirement-undo; S1 §7 conditional recovery register row.
- **文件(行号)**: `docs/gateflow/pa24-sharing-media-secret-manager-live-rotation-and-platform-acceptance-s1-implementation.md:98-100` and `:158`; plan `...-plan.md:81` and `:119`; `backend/src/main/java/com/onecall/core/sharing/internal/credential/MediaPlaybackCredentialAuthority.java:46-55`; `MediaPlaybackCredentialCodec.java:88`.
- **输入场景**: The operator completes B→C retirement after the approved "maximum outstanding credential/session/replay horizon from last possible K1 issuance" (S1 step 5/6), then Security authorizes a C→B recovery because K1 is not compromised/disabled/destroyed.
- **实际分支**: Step 7 restarts the complete cohort on "explicit fixed B" and requires "Re-run the applicable time-valid B overlap (old-K1 exact replay/verification) and fresh time-valid K2 issuance/verification checks for every purpose before reopening; expired or uncertain validity is INVALID/NOT EXECUTED and blocks reopening, not overlap success."
- **预期行为**: After full B retention, C retirement is declared only when no K1 credential/session/replay can still be outstanding. A C→B recovery should either (a) use the separately approved new immutable recovery bundle `K1 ACTIVE + unchanged K2 VERIFY_ONLY` and prove fresh K1 issuance/verification plus old-K2 verification, or (b) define a separately approved recovery evidence window/cohort; it should not require an A-issued old-K1 credential to be time-valid after the retirement horizon.
- **实际行为**: (1) S1 step 5 keeps B until the entire approved outstanding-credential/session/replay horizon has passed; step 6 then moves the whole cohort to C. Every A-issued K1 credential is therefore expired when C is reached. (2) Step 7's primary path restarts on "explicit fixed B" (K1 `VERIFY_ONLY`, K2 `ACTIVE`), so no fresh K1 credential can be issued there. (3) With expired K1 input, `verify` returns `Invalid` and replay returns `Malformed` because `expiresAt` is no longer after `now`; the S1 classifies this `INVALID`/`NOT EXECUTED` and says it blocks reopening. (4) The optional recovery bundle has K1 `ACTIVE` but K2 `VERIFY_ONLY`, so it also cannot satisfy the step-7 requirement for "fresh time-valid K2 issuance/verification". No single source configuration satisfies both the old-K1 time-valid overlap and fresh K2 issuance checks.
- **直接证据**: S1 `:98` (B retained until the entire horizon), `:99` (C after "full retention completion"), `:100` (C→B time-valid old-K1 overlap; expired blocks reopening), `:158` ("Validate both old credential populations and new K1 issuance before reopening"); plan `:81` ("The horizon governs safe production retention of B, not a promise that an A-issued evidence credential survives through C"); plan `:119` (C→B may restore K1 verification/replay with quiesce/drain but does not require a time-valid old-K1 overlap); authority `:46-47` (recorded kid absent → `Unavailable`), `:49`/`:55` (expired claim → `Malformed`), codec `:88` (`expiresAt.isAfter(now)` required).
- **影响**: The primary C→B recovery path cannot be executed as written: the operator must either keep traffic closed indefinitely, reopen while recording the required overlap as `INVALID`/`NOT EXECUTED` (a contract breach), or improvise an unapproved recovery bundle/cutover. The documented retirement-undo cannot prove its own acceptance state. This is a docs-only runbook defect; no product code change is implied.
- **建议改法和验证点**: Rewrite S1 §5 step 7 and §7 to separate post-retirement recovery evidence from pre-retirement B overlap: (a) make the `K1 ACTIVE + unchanged K2 VERIFY_ONLY` recovery bundle the required C→B configuration for reactivating K1 issuance, with fresh time-valid K1 issuance/verification and old-K2 verification; or (b) explicitly define an approved, feasible recovery evidence window/cohort and mark the old-K1 time-valid overlap `NOT EXECUTED` with an owner-approved waiver; or (c) classify old K1 at C→B as expired/key-absence evidence, not time-valid overlap. Add the corresponding disposition/owner to §7 and align plan §4 rollback text. Verification: read the revised step 7 and §7 against plan `:81`/`:119`; no `APPLICABLE` ambiguity; a reviewer must be able to state exactly which credential population proves each reopening check, and no step may require a credential that S1's own retention rule guarantees is expired.
- **修复风险（低/中/高）**: 低（runbook/owner text only; no Java/test/POM/CI change; requires Security/Sharing + Operations approval for the chosen recovery evidence path).
