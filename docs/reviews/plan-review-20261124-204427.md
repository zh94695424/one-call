## Findings

### 1-未修复-高-并发验收oracle依赖未声明的PostgreSQL延迟预算（§5 与 §8.2 互相矛盾）
- **位置**: §5 `docs/gateflow/pa41-customer-order-creation-foundation-plan.md:98`（`SET LOCAL statement_timeout=2000ms, lock_timeout=250ms, idle_in_transaction_session_timeout=5000ms as existing catalog command pattern`）、§5 step 1 (`:100`)、§5 step 5 (`:104`)、§5 末段 (`:111`, "Deadlock/timeout rollback maps 503; caller may retry")、§3.1 step 8 (`:68`)、§8.2 `:302` `concurrentSameKeyHasOneCreateAndOneReplay`、`:303` `concurrentChangedKeyPayloadHasOneWinnerAndConflict`、`:304` `unknownOrderBlocksNewCheckoutButNotCreationReplay`、`:315-316` 两个 serialize 测试、`:332` `lockOrderAndTimeoutsMatchExistingLifecycleAndCatalogKeys`（literal "2s250ms5s"）。
- **问题类型**: 契约缺失 / 并发恢复风险 / 不可直接实施
- **当前写法**: plan 同时钉死了 250ms 的锁等待上限和"被阻塞方必须确定性返回 201+200 / 201+409 / wait witnesses"的验收 oracle，但从未说明等待超过 250ms 时被阻塞方应返回什么，也没有给出避免该窗口的测试编排。
- **反例/失败场景**: 请求 A(201) 与同 subject、同 key 的请求 B 由同一个 latch 同时放行。B 的第一条语句就是 rank-3 subject advisory（`pg_advisory_xact_lock`），而 A 把它一直持有到 commit，所以 B 的等待时间 ≈ A 的**整个事务**时长（authority 读、catalog 重读、`idempotency_begin`、owner consent append、order insert、`idempotency_complete`、2×Outbox、2×audit，约 14 次 containerized PG16 往返外加 PL/SQL 函数体）。在满载 CI runner 上该跨度超过 250ms 完全现实，PostgreSQL 抛 55P03，`§3.1 step 8` 把它映射成 sanitized 503，钉死的 201/200（以及 `:303` 的 201/409、`:315`、`:316`）验收断言变红——偶发，但在 `mvn -Pcontainer-smoke clean verify` 全套负载下可复现。
- **为什么有问题**: 同一份 plan 的两条已钉死条款互相冲突，而且两条都是验收标准。250ms 是**运营命令路径**的约定：全仓扫描显示 `lock_timeout` 只在三处被设置——`backend/src/main/java/com/onecall/core/catalog_order/internal/operationscommand/JooqCatalogSaleLifecycleRepository.java:39`、`backend/src/main/java/com/onecall/core/catalog_order/internal/operationsdraft/JooqCatalogDraftRepository.java:42`、`backend/src/main/java/com/onecall/core/operations_iam/subjectread/OperationsReadContext.java:39`——全部是有界重试的 admin/operations 路径；没有任何面向客户的串行化写路径（subject claim、activation、share command）设置它。项目自己的并发测试做法是反过来**给故意阻塞的连接抬高上限**：`backend/src/test/java/com/onecall/archtests/container/PublicShareAdmissionIT.java:942`（'2500ms'）和 `backend/src/test/java/com/onecall/archtests/container/CustomerShareCommandsIT.java`（'10s'）。PA41 却把这个 fail-fast 上限移植到唯一需要用"被阻塞的同伴"来证明其核心不变量的地方。plan `§10.1` 又承认规划环境无法运行 Docker/PostgreSQL，所以这个延迟假设完全没有被测量过。
- **直接证据**: plan `:97-98/:100/:104/:105/:111/:68/:302-304/:315-316/:332`；`JooqCatalogSaleLifecycleRepository.java:38-50`；上述 `lock_timeout` 全仓三处扫描；`PublicShareAdmissionIT.java:931-960`；`docs/14-production-data-model-and-flyway-schema-spec.md:3338-3345`（§9.17 T-S2-07 冻结序列不含任何 timeout 条款，故 plan 的取值是从代码约定照搬而非设计来源）。
- **影响**: 实施 Agent 跑偏 / 生成错误代码 / review 不可验收 / 后续返工——实施者只能三选一：悄悄放宽一条钉死的验收断言（code review 必须拒）、偏离 §5 与 `:332` 钉死的超时值（plan 级决定，越权），或交付一套时红时绿的验收套件，让后续 slice 无法信任它。
- **建议改法和验证点**: 二选一并写进正文。(a) 给客户 checkout 路径采用客户写路径惯例：不设 `SET LOCAL lock_timeout`（保留 `statement_timeout`/`idle_in_transaction` 护栏），并把 `:332` 的 literal 更新为新值；或 (b) 保留 250ms，则把 `:302-304/:315-316` 重写为不变量 oracle：恰好一个 effect bundle（1 order / 1 consent / 1 completed PURCHASE / 2 Outbox / 2 audit），被阻塞方结果从明示集合 {200, 409, 503} 取值，用 `pg_locks`/`pg_stat_activity` 等待见证替代对钟点结果的期望，并追加"A commit 之后"有界同 key 复算必须得到文档化的 200/409 且零 delta。验证点：两个并发 IT 与两个 serialize IT 在全套负载下重复 ≥20 次且 `-Dsurefire.rerunFailingTestsCount=0`，完成报告须引用实测 winner 事务时长与所选上限的余量。
- **修复风险**: 低（纯文字；不动 schema/privilege/topology/contract）。
- **严重程度**: 高。

## Final Plan Review Conclusion

`fail`

Rationale. The plan is exceptionally well-evidenced and I confirmed that independently: all 25 §9.1 frozen raw hashes match the files on disk, the 1772→1864 identity arithmetic and the future digest `1f057c51…ed9e` reproduce exactly, the LO writable-template reuse precedent is real and already consumed cross-module, the frozen grants really do make the fail-closed/42501 assertions provable, the schema really can store every §10.19 snapshot fact, the needed reads are index-supported, and the PERSONAL-only versus frozen-contract mismatch is handled without changing contract semantics. Three candidate blockers I raised against this plan did not survive that testing and are recorded as refuted rather than as findings.

It nevertheless is not code-generation-ready as written, for two reasons that are internal to the plan rather than matters of taste. Finding 1 is a self-contradiction between two pinned acceptance clauses: §5's 250ms `lock_timeout` (an operations-only convention that no customer write path in this repository uses, and one the plan could not measure) versus §8.2's requirement that a transactionally blocked peer return exactly 200 or 409 — where the block duration is the winner's whole transaction. Finding 2 leaves the value of a mandatory stored column ambiguous between two encodings whose frozen length ceilings differ (475 vs 512), and the wrong-but-plausible reading breaks a CHECK constraint or the exact-key replay rule that the Goal Confirmation itself pins. Both are resolvable with plan text only: no schema, privilege, migration, topology, OpenAPI or scope change is required, and no redesign is implied. Findings 3-7 can be carried in the same revision as text plus explicit §10.2 residual rows with owners.

Required before re-gating: amend findings 1 and 2 (with the concurrency/timeout contract stated explicitly and the full INSERT tuple enumerated). Recommended in the same pass: findings 3-7 as text/residual rows. A delta re-review confined to §3.1, §5, §8.2 and the §10.2 table is sufficient once 1 and 2 are addressed.
