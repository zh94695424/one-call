- **建议改法和验证点**: Change the future values to `20/103 + 9/162 = 265`; update both CI constants (`ci.yml:162` and the exact-inventory `91`/`130`/`221` sites) and every README occurrence; add a small arithmetic table (`12+24+8=44`, `91+12=103`, `130+32=162`, `103+162=265`) and assert it mechanically.

### 06-未修复-[中]-Replay verification ordering contradicts T-S3-04: a same-key replay can be denied by a fresh verification before its witness is read
- **位置**: Plan §2.2 (lines 31-33) and §2.3 step 2 (line 42); compare docs/14 T-S3-04 steps 2-4.
- **问题类型**: 状态机漏洞 / 并发恢复风险 / 契约缺失
- **当前写法**: "Resolve the grant without a row lock, then perform mode verification outside the grant lock"; the transaction then locks the grant (step 1) and reads `(share_grant_id, admission_key)` (step 2). The LOCKED decision ("a new key on LOCKED returns 409") is written in step 1 before the step-2 key lookup.
- **反例/失败场景**: A lost-response retry with a valid committed admission but a changed/missing verification proof (or an unavailable proof provider) fails `403 SHARE_VERIFICATION_FAILED`/`503` before the replay witness is read, instead of returning the original admission and ticket. Likewise, an existing admission under a `LOCKED` grant can be denied as `SHARE_LOCKED` if step 1 applies the LOCKED decision without first resolving the key. docs/14 T-S3-04 step 2 explicitly says an existing durable key is resolved by steps 3-5 and "any pre-computed verification result is discarded on the replay path"; step 4 preserves replay while LOCKED.
- **为什么有问题**: The plan's ordering makes idempotent replay dependent on fresh proof success, which contradicts the frozen replay contract and the core lost-response/reconstruction goal. The ordering of the LOCKED decision is also ambiguous because the key is only read in the next step.
- **直接证据**: `docs/14-production-data-model-and-flyway-schema-spec.md:3410-3413` (T-S3-04 steps 2-4); plan lines `31-33,41-42`.
- **影响**: Valid replays denied; same-key retries after lost response fail; the `sameKeyReplay...`/`lostResponseRetry...` acceptance goals are not provable; LOCKED replay may be inconsistently implemented.
- **建议改法和验证点**: Perform a key/witness existence read before mode verification (or make verification lazy and discard its result on the replay path); explicitly order REVOKED/EXPIRED/LOCKED decisions after resolving the key; add a test for same-key replay with changed/unavailable proof and for LOCKED replay; verify no fresh policy/KDF call on replay.
- **修复风险**: 中
- **严重程度**: 中
