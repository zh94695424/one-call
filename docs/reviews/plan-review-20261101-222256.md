## 4. New findings

### R-B2-01 [Medium] The legacy JAR filename firewall already rejects eight of the eighteen approved coordinates

`.github/workflows/ci.yml:266` applies `^(…|spring-web|tomcat|…|spring-boot-(?:jdbc|jooq|flyway|test|web|security)|spring-boot-starter-(?:jdbc|jooq|flyway|web|security|data-)|…)`
to every `BOOT-INF/lib` name. Measured against the real resolved set, eight approved JARs hit it:
`spring-web-7.0.10.jar`, `spring-webmvc-7.0.10.jar`, `tomcat-embed-core/el/websocket-11.0.27.jar`,
`spring-boot-starter-webmvc-4.1.2.jar` (matched by the `spring-boot-starter-web` alternative), and
`spring-boot-webmvc-4.1.2.jar` plus `spring-boot-web-server-4.1.2.jar` (matched by `spring-boot-web`).
`docs/gateflow/pa11-customer-auth-api-foundation-plan.md:292` says "Do not loosen prefixes: legacy substring bans
remain effective for any coordinate outside the exact approved set" and simultaneously requires rejecting
"unapproved `spring-boot-starter-web`" — an instruction that cannot be satisfied literally, because the approved
`spring-boot-starter-webmvc` filename is a superset of that prefix. Two implementer readings are both bad: keep the
current `any(...)` scan (CI red on the correct build) or delete the prefixes (a real firewall loosening).
Related precision gap: the same bullet's ban on "Jackson 2 core/databind" is not expressible on filenames at all,
because Jackson 3 ships `jackson-core-3.1.7.jar`/`jackson-databind-3.1.7.jar` from
`tools.jackson.core` — identical filename shape, different group — and `com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-annotations:2.21`
is deliberately approved.
Required change (one or two sentences in §8): state that the negative regex is evaluated only on JAR names outside the
frozen approved filename set (exact filename equality, no wildcards), that the approved set therefore exempts those
eight coordinates by identity while still rejecting `spring-boot-starter-web`, `spring-websocket`,
`tomcat-embed-jasper`, `jakarta.servlet-api`, WebFlux/Netty and validation starters, and that Jackson 2 core/databind
is rejected group-wise through the resolved-coordinate comparison rather than by filename.
