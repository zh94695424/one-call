- **输入场景**: After a completed B→C cutover and logical K1 retirement, Security authorizes a C→B rollback because K1 is not compromised/disabled/prohibited and the prior retirement claim must be undone.
- **实际分支**: The runbook reaches step 7's C→B sentence: "C→B restores K1 verification/replay and undoes retirement; require explicit Security approval and K1 not compromised/disabled/prohibited; invalidate prior retirement claim." The step does not require quiescing/draining the cohort, stopping/removing all C-configured instances, restarting the complete cohort on B, or documenting the changed acceptance state.
- **预期行为**: Per accepted plan §4 rollback bullet (`:119`), C→B must be permitted only with explicit Security authorization, K1 not compromised/disabled/prohibited, **quiesce/drain**, and **documented changed acceptance state**. This is the same whole-cohort mixed-version hazard the S1 correctly applies to A→B step 4 (`:97`) and B→C step 6 (`:99`).
- **实际行为**: Step 7 is the only recovery/cutover transition that omits the explicit whole-cohort stop/restart/drain and changed-state record. The document has generic prerequisites in §3 `:54` ("whole-cohort stop/restart and drain procedure") and §7 `:157` ("Whole-cohort quiesce/drain, downtime, recovery and audit"), but the executable step does not bind them, unlike steps 4 and 6. An operator following step 7 alone could perform a rolling C→B rollback.
- **直接证据**: Plan `:119` explicitly requires "quiesce/drain and document the changed acceptance state" for C→B; S1 `:100` lists only explicit Security approval, K1 not compromised/disabled/prohibited, and invalidation of the prior retirement claim.
