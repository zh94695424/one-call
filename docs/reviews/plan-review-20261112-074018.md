- **Location:** fixed plan §8.3 line 357 (DatabaseRuntimeAcl bullet); source `backend/src/test/java/com/onecall/archtests/container/DatabaseRuntimeAclRbacHardeningIT.java:603-613`, called from `:160` (`v014FreshChainHasExactHistoryAndImmutablePredecessors`) and `:425` (`v014IsPrivilegeOnlyAndLeavesUnrelatedBoundariesEquivalent`).
