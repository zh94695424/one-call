## 8. Final plan review conclusion

**fail**

Rationale: the plan's factual and arithmetic core is unusually well verified (see section 2 and section 4), and the authorized two-column ACL delta itself is minimal, intra-module and implementable. Finding 01 is decisive: two files the plan explicitly forbids editing (`PostgresContainerIT`, `DatabaseRuntimeAclRbacHardeningIT`) are guaranteed to fail from V018 plus the PA25 four-input/exact-408/`logins=7 effective_roles=9 memberships=7` change, five more current-state boundary tests are missing from the write set, and the plan's own section 8 validation commands therefore cannot return the "zero failures/errors/skips" it requires - while section 9 states there is no blocking open question. Finding 02 is decisive in the other direction: the identity topology PA36 is told to ship cannot serve the single shared pool it is wired to, so following the new runbook would break already-merged PA33/PA34 endpoints (503) and, absent a consumer, the slice also hands a new authenticatable credential reach into employee-IAM and IVR writes. Findings 03-05 are additional correctness/robustness defects that would be discovered only during implementation. The correct next step is a plan fix-1 that resolves the write-set/forbidden-edit contradiction and returns the LO-vs-shared-pool identity decision to goal confirmation; findings 06-07 can be absorbed in that same fix or explicitly deferred with owners.
