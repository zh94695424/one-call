## 4. Adversarial lens analysis

### Lens 1 — Goal drift and overengineering

The proposed call-task snapshot and state machine (8 task statuses, 7 IVR actions, 6 task-match values, 14 call-progress states, 4 upload states, 4 evidence states, plus append-only events with `eventId`/monotonic time/allowed order, idempotency keys, and `revision`) is larger than a naive prototype needs. However:

- The binding goal (control document "Binding goal confirmation"; task "Confirmed goal") **explicitly enumerates** "dialer launch, inbound wait, claim, B-leg outbound, ringing, answered, bridged, recording, call end, upload and evidence processing are separate facts." The progress states are therefore goal-mandated, not invented.
- Task statuses match production doc 13 §4.2 exactly (`PENDING/CLAIMED/COMPLETED/FAILED/EXPIRED/REPLACED/CANCELLED/REJECTED`). Trimming them would misrepresent enums the prototype must not contradict.
- The plan **correctly drops** production extras: `UsageReservation`, `authorizationExpiresAt`, HMAC caller indexes, mTLS/signing/nonces, billing/权益预占, provider internals, and configuration-change modeling (`CALL_TASK_TTL_SECONDS = 30` is frozen; no helper accepts a caller TTL).
- Event-sourcing with idempotent `eventId` matches the existing house style (business `processedEvents` with `inputFingerprint`), not a new pattern.

**No drift.** Every surface change maps to a control-document success signal. The plan's own §11.1 "Over-modeling" risk and its mitigation ("keep only the states needed… do not add reservations, billing, retries or provider internals") are appropriate and consistent with what §4 actually specifies.

### Lens 2 — Contract boundary

Keeping call-task data outside the strict business snapshot **preserves isolation without creating two competing truths or bypassing generated-block rules**:

- `validateState` (`:645-718`) rejects any key outside `BUSINESS_FIELDS` or in `FORBIDDEN_IMPORT_KEYS`. Adding task/call arrays would force `schemaVersion` 2 plus a migration and full revalidation of the accepted validator — both material risks the plan avoids.
- The account-directory precedent is real and verified (`:96-144`, exported `:1112-1114`): a separate read-only fixture domain with its own exact-keys validator, outside `BUSINESS_FIELDS`. The call-task domain mirrors this.
- Bidirectional isolation is specified and testable: §7.1 item 9 (unit) and §7.2 item 8 (cross-channel) both require proving that importing a call-task snapshot never changes the business snapshot and vice versa.
- New exports (`CALL_TASK_TTL_SECONDS`, `labels.ownership`, `createCallTaskDemoState`, `callTaskSnapshot`, `loadCallTaskSnapshot`, and the mutation functions) go into `shared/onecall-business.js` and are regenerated into all three sources by the builder. The plan forbids hand-edits between the markers and hand-edits of `preview.html`, matching `build-prototypes.js` behavior. No generated-block bypass.
- `labels` is a deepFreeze object (`:100-110`); adding `ownership` before freeze is straightforward and consistent.

A two-truths residual exists (F-05: `call-task.evidenceStatus` vs `business.evidence` are not cross-validated), but this matches the current architecture (static Web calls already decoupled from `business.evidence`) and is labeled a local fixture. **Accept the separation**, as the plan's §11.3 open question requests.

### Lens 3 — State correctness

Correct against production doc 13 semantics within prototype scope:

- **Task vs no-task**: `DIAL_TASK_CALLEE` + `calleeConfirmation=NONE` vs `ENTER_CALLEE` + `DTMF_REQUIRED → DTMF_CONFIRMED`; `confirmDirectCallee` rejects any non-null `taskId` (§4.5). Neither UI nor QA blurs them (§7.2 asserts task path does not contain "DTMF"; landline asserts no "自动外呼").
- **30-second expiry**: frozen at create; claim boundary tested at `expiresAt-1ms` (succeeds) and `expiresAt`/later (fails); expired task's historical callee is never returned (§7.1 item 4). Matches doc 13 §5 and §10 (29.999 s / 30 s).
- **Replacement/cancel/claim competition**: replace and cancel only for own `PENDING`; concurrent resolve yields `REJECT_CALL_IN_PROGRESS`; same `callId` returns the first decision without re-claim; atomic claim leaves prior state byte-identical on rejection (§4.5, §7.1 items 3/5).
- **Dialer launch vs connection**: `dialerLaunchedAt` is a task field, not a call event; it cannot advance task/call progress or create evidence; UI shows "尚未接通" until `ANSWERED` (§2.3, §5.1.3). Matches doc 13 §1.7 and §9.1.
- **Upload vs evidence**: separate statuses; `EVIDENCE_READY` requires upload `RECEIVED` and passage through `EVIDENCE_PROCESSING` (§4.3, §4.5 item 7). Matches doc 13 §7.
- **Failures and immutable facts**: pre-bridge failure leaves `recordingSeconds=0`, upload `NOT_STARTED`, evidence `NOT_CREATED`; events are append-only, monotonic, allowed-order (§4.3, §4.5 item 7).

Minor: two IVR decision models coexist without a shared resolve (F-08) — a prototype fidelity trade-off, not a state-machine defect.

### Lens 4 — Surface fit

- **Mini may create and query**: `createCallTask`, `cancelCallTask`, explicit replace (`replacePending:true`), `launch-dialer`, `callStatus` page, snapshot/load hooks. Fits.
- **Web read-only**: no input/button in the diagnostic panel, no `tel:`/browser dial, no new route, auditor has no calls route (verified). Fits.
- **Operations read-only diagnostics**: no `data-action` inside `#call-diagnostics`; review selectors only set `state.reviewCallScenario` and navigate to the existing `/ivr-sessions`; admin+normal import guard preserved; twelve scenarios unchanged. Fits.
- **Account/workspace isolation**: tasks carry `ownerType/ownerId/workspaceId`; `CT-DEMO-0201` (ACCOUNT-DEMO-002) is hidden from account-001 Mini/Web and visible in Operations; the cross-workspace one-nonterminal-task invariant matches docs/11 §1. Fits, with the no-task-call derivation caveat (F-02).
- **Navigation conventions**: Mini adds one index button (62→63, with QA/README/docs 06/10 updated); Operations and Web add no route. Fits.

### Lens 5 — Slice size

One cross-surface slice is the right call. The shared fixture, three surface projections and cross-channel QA must agree at a single acceptance boundary; splitting by surface would require temporary divergent fixtures, duplicate vocabulary and two reviews of the same 30-second/claim semantics; splitting the shared contract from the surfaces would leave no runnable acceptance. This is a **behavior slice**, not mechanical file/owner slicing, and matches prior work-unit patterns (the S1/S2/S3 cross-channel unit was likewise treated as one behavior with surface sub-changes). The slice is large but atomic. **Accept one slice.**

### Lens 6 — QA truth

Good.
