### PR-35-A1 final state: fixed (documentation closure verified against merged source/tests)

The accepted Medium finding — after full B retention every A-issued K1 credential is expired, fixed B cannot issue fresh K1, and recovery bundle R cannot issue fresh K2, so the former single C→B step could not simultaneously require time-valid old-K1 overlap and fresh K2 — is closed by two distinct, feasible contracts:

1. **Fixed-B post-C source restoration** (S1 §4 `:72`, §5 recovery rows `:100–:103`, step 7a `:117`, §7 `:177`):
   - Requires independent per-instance attestation of current B source/version, complete valid bundle (K2 ACTIVE + same K1 VERIFY_ONLY), K1 presence/continuity, protected manifest and every instance/artifact/effective configuration.
   - Requires fresh **time-valid K2** issuance/verification before reopening; no fresh K1 is possible under B.
   - After compliant full retention, old A-issued K1 inputs are expired. Their verify `Invalid` / replay `Malformed` outcomes are recorded only as **source/precondition expiry observations** — explicitly not `VALID-TIME OVERLAP`, not `KEY-ABSENCE`, not reopening evidence.
   - When no time-valid K1 remains, the old-K1 `VALID-TIME OVERLAP` row is `NOT EXECUTED` and **does not block** fixed-B source restoration; a genuinely still-valid K1 may be checked as overlap. Unknown validity is `UNKNOWN`, never assumed expiry or pass.
2. **K1 issuance reactivation** (S1 §4 `:73`, §5 recovery rows `:104–`:105, step 7b `:119`, §7 `:178`):
   - Requires separately approved immutable **R** (`K1 ACTIVE + unchanged K2 VERIFY_ONLY`), whole-cohort quiesce/drain/complete restart and per-instance manifest verification.
   - Requires fresh **time-valid K1** issuance/verification before reopening; R cannot issue fresh K2.
   - Old K2 verification is recorded as `VALID-TIME OVERLAP` only while genuinely time-valid; after expiry/unknown its row is `NOT EXECUTED`, with no blocking overlap-pass claim. Expired old-K1 overlap is not required.
   - R is not fixed B, never an in-place B edit and not a fourth mandatory stage; the recovery mirror has no in-repo oracle and remains external `NOT EXECUTED`.
