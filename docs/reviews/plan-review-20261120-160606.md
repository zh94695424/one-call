## Open Questions

1. **Monotonic `versionNo` vs docs14.** Plan §2.2 explicitly declines any max-version scan and says positive `versionNo` need not exceed historical versions, but `docs/14-...md:1100` describes "Positive monotonic version within the product." The frozen OpenAPI/DB impose only `versionNo>=1` + `(product_id,version_no)` uniqueness. Confirm the Controller accepts the plan's non-enforced-monotonic interpretation (create `versionNo=5` while `10` exists is legal) rather than requiring a monotonic check. Evidence needed before implementation, not a blocker if the non-monotonic reading is affirmed.
2. **`pa*_start_*` formula enumeration.** Plan §8 item 2 states the principle "subtract PA39 identities when reconstructing each older historical baseline," but does not enumerate the ~20 sites in `ci.yml` (`pa38_start_totals`, all older `paXX_start_totals`, every `paXX_start_line_scan_total` substitution, and the `additions` union). Omitting `pa39_names` from any older formula silently shifts `pa38_start_totals`/etc. and breaks the historical-baseline assertions. Recommend adding an explicit checklist item; currently a `needs-evidence` precision gap rather than a proven defect.
3. **FK referential-action privilege for `created_by_employee_id`.** V004 grants `catalog_order_role` no SELECT on `operations_iam.employee_account`; PA39 create is the first runtime INSERT of `catalog_version.created_by_employee_id`. The shipped `activation_consent_role`→`service_activation_transition.actor_employee_id` insert is a strong precedent that RI checks do not require caller SELECT, but the plan should still require the live PG16 `createAndReplaceUseExactLocalRoleSequenceAndWitness` to prove an actual insert under the non-owner login, and treat a failure as a stop (ACL change) rather than a workaround.
4. **ACL "42 tuples" definition.** The arithmetic reproduces 42 under the plan's stated decomposition; the exact oracle (which grants count as tuples) must be reconstructed by implementation exactly as the plan demands, since a differing decomposition would change the assertion even if all real grants are present.

These are bounded, low-risk fixes to the plan's lockstep/edit-surface section, not a structural redesign; a focused plan fix that closes Findings 1–2 with exact literals should enable re-review.
